
=== gfm mode, example 608 (Autolinks) ===
--- markdown
< https://foo.bar >

--- expected (spec)
<p>&lt; https://foo.bar &gt;</p>

--- actual (renderer)
<p>&lt; <a href="https://foo.bar" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">https://foo.bar</a> &gt;</p>

--- expected normalized
<p>&lt; https://foo.bar &gt;</p>
--- actual normalized
<p>&lt; <a href="https://foo.bar">https://foo.bar</a> &gt;</p>
